How it works
01

Scan your Mac

Open the app and click Scan. In about a minute DrivePurge checks every folder on your disk and adds up how much space each one is using. There is nothing to set up and no account to create.

02

See where your space went

Instead of a long list of file names, you get a simple picture of your disk: the bigger the block, the more space that folder is using. Click any block to look inside it, and click Back to come out again.

03

Clean up safely

Point at anything you want gone and click Clean. Everything you select is listed for review first and then moved to your Trash, so nothing disappears by accident. DrivePurge never touches system files or anything your Mac needs to run.
